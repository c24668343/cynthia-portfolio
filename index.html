Predicting Problematic Internet Use in Children ↗

Data Mining & Applications, NTHU · Team of 4

Predicted a child's Severity Impairment Index from wrist-worn accelerometer time series and tabular health data that was high-dimensional, imbalanced and full of missing values.

  • Pruned features by correlation, added interaction terms (BMI × Age, Internet Hours × Age), compressed inputs with an autoencoder and imputed gaps with KNN (k = 5).
  • Trained LightGBM, XGBoost, CatBoost, TabNet, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting with stratified 5-fold CV and Optuna; tuned thresholds with Nelder–Mead to maximize QWK.
  • Combined three ensembles by majority vote — best QWK 0.53, final test QWK 0.492.
Python LightGBM XGBoost CatBoost TabNet Optuna
